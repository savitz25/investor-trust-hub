# Product principles

## Mission

Research before you invest.

InvestorTrustHub is an independent consumer research and decision-intelligence platform. It helps a person investigate investment professionals, firms, fees, retirement assumptions, funds, and eventually products.

## Philosophy

We organize the evidence. The consumer decides.

The product should feel sophisticated enough for someone with a large portfolio and simple enough for someone opening a first IRA. Calm, evidence-driven, high trust. Not CNBC. Not crypto. Not a bank vault. Not a dark “quant” terminal.

## Questions we exist to support

1. Who am I trusting?
2. Is this person or firm properly registered?
3. What does the regulatory record say?
4. What am I paying?
5. What do I actually own?
6. What assumptions drive my retirement plan?
7. What should I investigate before making a financial decision?

## Evidence over verdicts

- Show source text and identifiers.
- Use the status vocabulary. Do not invent a cleaner story.
- A verified official source is not an endorsement.
- Conflicting sources stay visible.

## No proprietary score

There is no Trust Score, Advisor Score, safety score, star ranking, or recommended-advisor badge. If derived analytics appear later, methodology and underlying evidence must be visible.

## Independence

No paid rankings. No lead-generation marketplace attached to research records. No featured placement that looks like research.

## Audience tone

Write like a careful research editor. Short sentences. No hype. No “this advisor is best for you.”
