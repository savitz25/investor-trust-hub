# Maine securities source audit

Audit baseline: `origin/main` at `a7c08a032990adafee8c79f80655f78726b8a568`; official source review checked 2026-10-12.

## Regulator and populations

The Maine Office of Securities administers the Maine Uniform Securities Act. Its licensing and registration sources distinguish investment advisers, federal covered advisers, investment adviser representatives, broker-dealers, agents, branch offices, and securities registration/exemption filings. Maine materials identify Form ADV/IARD for investment-adviser filings and Form BD / Form U4 through CRD for broker-dealers and agents. Maine branch filings use Form BR through CRD. Firms, people, and offices are separate regulatory grains.

IAPD and FINRA BrokerCheck support public identity lookup but do not replace jurisdictional registration. State IA, federal covered IA notice filing, and ERA status remain distinct. Principal-office geography alone is not proof of Maine registration. No identity is joined to an adverse proceeding by name only.

## Acquisition state

No dated IARD/CRD releases, Maine jurisdiction-specific firm/person/branch rows, ERA snapshot, or normalized enforcement attachment were acquired for this publication. Counts by class and source clock are therefore `NOT_ACQUIRED`, not zero. Maine's public licensing guidance documents available search paths and distinctions; it is not a counted census. No combined total, duplicate-firm estimate, or score is asserted.
